## A. Bugs (tested on my Chromebook)

1. **White strobe outline around scenery: still there for most of the game.** v0.8 removed
   multisampling on the glow pass's HDR target (the leading theory), but it didn't fix it on
   my Chromebook. Something else is causing it. Don't guess again; make it findable:
   - **Strobe test mode** (`?strobetest`). Cycles one layer off at a time every 6 seconds:
     post-processing, glow, shadows, valley mist, wind sway, render scale 1.0, near/far tree
     swap frozen. Show a large on-screen label of what's off right now, so I can just walk
     and watch, then tell you which label made it stop.
   - Show the GPU name in the `?debug` panel (WEBGL_debug_renderer_info), so I can report it.
   - Meanwhile, check the remaining suspects in the code and fix any that are clearly wrong:
     - Shadow acne or peter-panning on flat-shaded and double-sided materials (bias and
       normalBias against the 120 m / 2048 shadow map).
     - Depth fighting at silhouettes. Check the near/far planes in play and on the summit.
     - FXAA on a Chromebook GPU: edge detection on bright snow and sky.
     - Fog or sky showing through thin gaps between faces.
     - Anything that changes every frame and toggles visibility or material state.
   - Report what each suspect showed and what you changed. Say plainly what can only be
     confirmed on the Chromebook.
2. **Lag when the climber starts moving.** There's a short delay between pressing a key and
   the climber moving. Measure the time from keydown to first movement, and find out whether
   it's acceleration, input handling, or a frame hitch: shader compiles the first time
   something is drawn, or the first near-tree swap. Fixes may include:
   - compiling shaders up front (`renderer.compileAsync` during the start transition)
   - a snappier first step: a faster start of the acceleration curve, same top speed and pacing

   Ascent times must not change.
3. **Trees pop in.** Some trees go from invisible to visible right in front of the camera.
   Trees should never be invisible. Find the cause; candidates:
   - the per-chunk near/far swap and its v0.8 hysteresis
   - stale bounding spheres on instanced chunks (frustum culling)
   - the far version's position, scale or rotation not matching the near one (a tree "appears"
     in a different spot)
   - chunk size and when chunks get built

   Then fix it so near/far changes are invisible: matching positions, plus a short dithered
   cross-fade or a per-instance swap distance instead of whole chunks.
4. **Summit cairn and flag overlap.** The flag pole sits at a fixed height inside the rebuilt
   cairn, so the flag overlays the stones. Plant the pole in the top of the cairn: base inside
   the top stone, height from the cairn's real top, flag clear above. Restyle both to look
   natural:
   - the cairn: a broad, solid summit cairn of stacked local stones, balanced like the trail
     cairns, a bit wider than tall
   - the flag: a weathered pole wedged between stones, a smaller flag that moves in the wind

## B. Refinements

5. **Lower summit celebration camera.** The orbit is high and far. Bring it down near eye
   level (just above the climber's head). Keep a gentle orbit and the reveal of the far range,
   but frame the climber, cairn and horizon rather than looking down on the crater.
6. **A clear side trail to the lake.** I didn't notice a side trail to the lake at all, only
   the original path. Make it obvious:
   - a full-width, well-worn trail branching from the main trail at a clear junction (same
     tread as the main trail, not the narrower ribbon it has now)
   - a large trail signpost at the junction with an arrow and distance ("Obsidian Tarn ↗
     0.1 mi · water")
   - trees and rocks cleared so the lake is visible from the junction
   - cairns along it above treeline (kept), and a small open spot at the shore by the
     viewpoint sign

   It counts as on-trail. The main route and gem positions stay the same.
7. **Performance budget.** Bring the meadow (+10%) and tarn/sunrise (+17%) views back to the
   v0.6.1 triangle budget, and draw calls to within +5%. Report draw calls, triangles and
   frame time per view (v0.6.1 / v0.8 / v0.9).
8. **Keep improving trees and rocks** where the budget allows. They're "starting to look
   better": push species character a bit further and check them up close and at a distance
   in the kit preview.

## Roadmap (don't build now, but don't make it harder)

- Two-page start: route and conditions, then character (male/female climber) and gear, with
  stats that scale to RPG-style progression.
- Alternative routes on Cindercrest.
- Seasons and weather.
- Several mountains in one range, then more ranges, each inspired by real mountains and ranges.

Build assets and recipes with this in mind:
- Kit items stay generic and settings-driven.
- Range-level settings (palette, rock types, tree species mix, snow line) can be shared by
  several mountain recipes.
- Nothing mountain-specific is hard-coded in the kit or scene code (e.g. the lake, summit
  cairn and trail signs should come from the mountain's recipe or route data).

Note any places in the current code that would block this and should be refactored later.
